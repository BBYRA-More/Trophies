Challenge Cup

Sneakbox Fleet - Manasquan River Yacht Club / BBYRA Race

The Challenge Cup is the legacy of a five-year series of annual three-race regattas between the Belmar Yacht Club and Manasquan River Yacht Club beginning in 1906, retired to MRYC after a tie-breaking regatta in 1910. The sterling silver trophy was tooled by Tiffany & Co., New York at a cost of $250. A base was added and the trophy used for an open Sneakbox regatta for many years. In 2003 it was rededicated to the winner of the Sneakbox fleet at the MRYC-hosted BBYRA regatta.

Carl Ill Memorial Trophy

Junior Fleet (various classes) - Mantoloking Yacht Club

The silver tray is mounted on a stand built by Philip S. Clarke Jr. using white cedar from Henry Tomlinson's boat Queen Ann, the altar platform of All Saints Church in Bay Head (dating to 1883), and an oak cross-tie from the Point Pleasant–Bay Head Trolley line (laid 1903, discontinued 1920, tracks removed 1923 - this piece was dug up in front of Applegate's Hardware in 1972). The silver tray was given in memory of Dr. Carl Ill, a past Commodore of the Mantoloking Yacht Club. The trophy is always raced for by a junior class, spanning Sneakbox (1952–1967), M-16 (1968–1976), Laser M (1977–1979), and Laser Radial (1986–2001).

Yachtsman's Club of Philadelphia Trophy

'B' Sneakbox / Laser 'M' / Laser Radial - Now held by MYC

Originally a Lavallette Yacht Club trophy for 'B' Sneakboxes, the Yachtsman's Trophy passed permanently to Mantoloking after Paulding Phelps won it three times (1946–1948). MYC has continued to race it in the junior class across multiple fleets. Full winners history is shared with the Lavallette section above.

“In Memory of William C. Kalning” Trophy

Lightning Fleet - Metedeconk River Yacht Club Host Day

The Bill Kalning Trophy was donated by James G. Carson in 1973 for the winner of the Lightning race on the day Metedeconk River Yacht Club hosts or co-hosts the BBYRA regatta. It honors William C. Kalning for dedicated service to the Barnegat Bay Yacht Racing Association. The trophy is kept at MCYC; a keeper trophy is presented to the winner each year. MCYC sailors dominated throughout, with Paul Gelenitis winning 13 times and Jim Carson winning multiple years.
